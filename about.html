About Me Hero Section

Hi I'm Jo 👋

I’m a Filipina-Canadian Graphic & Motion Designer with a love for visual storytelling, thoughtful composition, and design that moves people.
Driven by curiosity and a passion for problem-solving, I thrive at the intersection of creativity and intention. Whether I’m designing layered brand visuals, animating dynamic motion pieces, or crafting immersive 3D scenes, I approach every project with care, precision, and a strong sense of narrative. To me, design is more than aesthetics; it’s about creating moments that feel meaningful, engaging, and alive.
I’m equal parts introvert and extrovert: I love quiet mornings with a good book just as much as collaborative brainstorms with a great team. You’ll often find me exploring a new trail, curled up with a novel, or immersed in a video game world (controller in hand, snacks nearby). I believe in the power of thoughtful design, human connection, and never settling for “good enough.”
I bring a human-centred, detail-driven approach to every collaboration, grounded in curiosity and a genuine love for what I do. I’m here to make things beautiful, expressive, impactful, and to do it with heart.

Let's build something meaningful.
