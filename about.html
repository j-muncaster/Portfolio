About Me Hero Section

Hi I'm Jo 👋

I’m a Filipina-Canadian UX | UI Designer and Front-End Developer with a deep love for clean design, clever code, and a well-told story.
Driven by curiosity and a passion for problem-solving, I thrive at the intersection of logic and creativity. Whether I’m perfecting a user flow, crafting responsive interfaces, or digging into code until everything just works, I bring care, precision, and elegance to every project I touch. Design isn’t just a job to me — it’s a puzzle I’m always excited to solve.
I’m equal parts introvert and extrovert: I love quiet mornings with a good book, just as much as I love lively brainstorming sessions with a great team. You’ll often find me outdoors exploring a new trail, curled up with a novel, or immersed in a video game world (controller in hand, snacks nearby). I believe in the power of thoughtful design, human connection, and never settling for “good enough.”
I bring a thoughtful, human-centred approach to every collaboration; one grounded in trust, curiosity, and a genuine love for what I do. I'm here to make things beautiful and functional, and to do it with heart.

Let's build something meaningful.
